# IT 140 Academic Advisor Support Guide

This guide is the starting point for **Academic Advisors** supporting students who are enrolled in, preparing for, or experiencing difficulty in **IT 140 - Introduction to Scripting**.

Advisors often have a longer relationship with a student than any individual course instructor, so a student may contact their advisor first when something goes wrong in IT 140.

You do **not** need to be a programmer or technical-support specialist to help effectively.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> The advisor role is to **set realistic course expectations, recognize the type of problem, help the student understand the next step, and connect the student to the right support resource**.
>
> Advisors are not expected to troubleshoot Git, Python, VS Code, GitHub, or IT 140 course automation.

This guide is written for both STEM and non-STEM advisors. IT 140 serves as an introductory programming course for STEM students and may also be taken by students from other academic areas as an elective.

## IT 140 in 60 Seconds

- **Programming language:** Python.
- **Length:** Eight weeks.
- **Credit/load expectation:** A three-credit course in this format should require **about 16 hours per week on average for an average student** under SNHU and applicable accreditation expectations.
- **Prior experience:** Previous programming experience is not required, but students who are new to programming may need more than the average amount of time.
- **Pace:** Concepts build from week to week. Early delays can make later work more difficult.
- **Weekly approach:** Students should work across multiple sessions during the week rather than waiting until the weekend to begin the week's work.
- **Learning platform:** zyBooks is a significant part of the weekly workload, especially early in the course.
- **Development environment:** The Codio Virtual Desktop (CVD) is the reference environment; supported local setup is optional.
- **Help:** Instructor support, 24/7 Drop-In Tutoring with Python support, and IT 140-focused Academic Support live sessions are available.

For the shared course background, see [IT 140 Course Overview](../shared/course-overview.md).

## The Most Important Advisor Distinction

When a student says:

> "IT 140 isn't working."

the problem may mean very different things.

### Technical Problem

Examples:

- the CVD will not open;
- VS Code will not start;
- Python cannot run;
- GitHub authentication fails; or
- the student cannot create or clone the repository.

**Route:** IT Service Desk.

### Learning Problem

Examples:

- the student does not understand a programming concept;
- the student's code runs but gives the wrong result;
- the student needs help debugging; or
- the student needs more practice.

**Route:** Faculty and/or Academic Support.

### Assignment or Grading Problem

Examples:

- what must be submitted;
- whether work meets the rubric;
- a grade;
- instructor feedback; or
- an assignment deadline.

**Route:** Faculty.

### Academic or Persistence Concern

Examples:

- the student is overwhelmed;
- the student is considering changing their academic plan;
- the student is concerned about course sequencing;
- the student is balancing the course with broader academic or life circumstances.

**Route:** Advisor, using normal advising processes, while connecting course-specific subproblems to the appropriate support role.
